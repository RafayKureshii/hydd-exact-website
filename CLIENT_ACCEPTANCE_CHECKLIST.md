# HYDD Original-Site Acceptance Checklist

Do not mark production-ready until all items have evidence (screenshots/test reports) and client approval.

- [ ] All original public French URLs captured and match the live site visually at desktop and mobile sizes.
- [ ] All images, fonts, videos, navigation effects, responsive menus, and original interactions work.
- [ ] WhatsApp opens the approved `+33 7 65 84 95 46` account with page-aware prefilled text; existing WhatsApp links are not obscured.
- [ ] `HYDD_TRANSLATIONS.json` has complete reviewed EN and AR content; no French text accidentally remains untranslated in main content.
- [ ] EN and AR layout match the French visual styling where feasible, while Arabic reads RTL with proper alignment and wrapping.
- [ ] All hreflang/canonical tags, nav links, sitemap, old URLs and redirects verified; no unapproved SEO/content changes.
- [ ] Existing Squarespace forms and other server-driven features independently rebuilt and E2E tested where necessary.
- [ ] SMTP delivery, spam protection, privacy/consent, cookies and analytics tested if used.
- [ ] IP/asset rights and live-site access verified by the client.
- [ ] HTTPS, backups, monitoring, rollout and rollback tested on client's server.
- [ ] Client visually approves final site BEFORE DNS is redirected away from Squarespace.
